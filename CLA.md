This Agreement applies only when Contributor affirmatively accepts it through a documented process approved by the Company. The Company should retain a record of the accepted Agreement and its version, together with the Contributor's identity and acceptance date.

For contributions made on behalf of an employer or other legal entity, the Company should obtain acceptance from an authorised representative of that entity or use a separate entity contributor agreement.
